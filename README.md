Planning to work, study, live, or conduct business in the UAE? Indian certificates and official documents may need authentication and attestation before they can be used for official purposes in the United Arab Emirates. 
What Is UAE Certificate Attestation?

UAE certificate attestation is the process of verifying the authenticity of documents issued in India for use in the UAE.

Depending on the document and its purpose, the process may involve authentication in India, MEA authentication, attestation through the relevant UAE mission, and UAE-side MoFA attestation.

UAE Certificate Attestation Process

The exact process depends on the document type and issuing authority. A typical process may include:

Document verification – Check the certificate and determine the applicable procedure.
State-level authentication – Authentication by the relevant Indian authority, where applicable.
MEA authentication – Process the document through the Ministry of External Affairs, India, where applicable.
UAE Mission attestation – Submit the authenticated document through the applicable UAE mission.
UAE MoFA attestation – Complete UAE-side attestation when required.

Always verify the latest requirements before submitting original documents because procedures can vary.

Degree Certificate Attestation for UAE Jobs

Professionals moving from India to the UAE may need degree certificate attestation for employment. Employers, licensing authorities, or government departments may request authenticated educational qualifications.

Applicants should confirm the specific requirements for their profession and employment process.

UAE Attestation for Students

Students may require attestation of educational documents when applying to universities or educational institutions in the UAE.

Depending on the institution, documents can include school certificates, higher secondary certificates, diplomas, degrees, transcripts, and other academic records.
